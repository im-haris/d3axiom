Why most outreach is ignored

Decision-makers receive generic pitch messages every day and ignore almost all of them. Outreach works when it is targeted, relevant and personal: the right people, a real reason to talk, and patient follow-up. That takes time, which is exactly what most founders do not have.

How we generate leads

1. Define the target. We agree a narrow ideal customer profile: industry, company size, location and job titles.

2. Build the list. We research and verify decision-makers who match the profile, prioritising people who are active on LinkedIn.

3. Write the messages. Short, personal connection requests and follow-ups, approved by you before anything is sent.

4. Run the outreach. Steady, human-paced outreach that respects platform limits and keeps your account safe.

5. Hand over the conversations. When a prospect is interested, we pass the conversation to you or book the meeting directly.
