Why sellers hand over their Amazon accounts

Running an Amazon business means juggling advertising, listings, stock, promotions, reports and account health, every single week. Most founders end up reacting to problems instead of growing the brand. Account management gives that daily workload to a specialist, so your time goes into product, sourcing and strategy.

What working with us looks like

One dedicated specialist. A dedicated Amazon specialist is allocated to your account. You always know who is working on your brand, and you talk to the same person every week.

Profit, not just sales. We track real margins after fees, ad spend and cost of goods. Growth that loses money is not growth, so every plan starts with what each product can afford to spend.

A plan for every quarter. Each quarter we agree priorities: which products to push, which campaigns to launch, which deals to run, and what to prepare for the next peak season.

Automation where it helps. Repetitive work such as search-term harvesting, bleeder checks and reporting is handled with our own tools, which leaves more time for decisions that need judgement.

You approve the big decisions. New campaigns, budget changes and promotions are approved by you first, and we prepare detailed reviews whenever you ask.

Questions

Amazon Account Management FAQs.

What is included in Amazon account management?

Advertising, listing optimization, deals and promotions, account health checks, margin tracking and a weekly report, managed by a dedicated Amazon specialist.

Do you need full access to my Seller Central?

We start with read-only reports. Wider access is only added after you approve a written plan, and you can remove it at any time.

Is there a long contract?

No. Management runs month to month and can be cancelled before the next billing cycle.

Can you work with my existing team?

Yes. We can manage the whole account or focus on the parts your team does not have time for, such as advertising and reporting.
