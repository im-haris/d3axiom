Questions sellers ask first

Before you book.

Am I hiring one person or an agency?

You talk to Haris on every call and every report. Specialists in PPC, design, development and video do the production work under his review.

What does the free audit include?

A review of your campaigns, listings and account health, with a ranked list of fixes. There is no obligation to continue.

Do you need full access to my Seller Central?

No. We start with read-only reports. Write access is added only when you approve the plan.

Which markets do you work in?

US, UK, EU and Australia on Amazon, plus Walmart, eBay, TikTok Shop and Shopify.

Can I hire you only for a website or a video?

Yes. Design, development and video projects are scoped and priced on their own.

How is the work priced?

Amazon management is a monthly retainer. Builds and videos are fixed-price per project. You get the number before any work starts.
