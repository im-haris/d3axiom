How it works

Your effective bid is the base bid raised by the placement adjustment. To hit your target, the effective bid has to move by the ratio of target ACoS to current ACoS. If that would need an adjustment below 0%, which Amazon does not allow, the base bid has to come down instead. If it needs more than 900%, the placement is at Amazon’s cap.

factor = target ACoS ÷ current ACoS new adjustment = (1 + current adjustment) × factor − 1 effective bid = base bid × (1 + adjustment)

How it works

Breakeven ACoS is your profit per sale before advertising, as a share of the price. Spend more than that on ads per sale and you lose money. You pay for every click but only some convert, so multiplying by your conversion rate turns an allowable cost per sale into an allowable cost per click. Many calculators skip this step.

profit = price − cost of goods − referral fee − FBA fee breakeven ACoS = profit ÷ price target ACoS = breakeven ACoS − margin to keep max CPC = breakeven ACoS × price × conversion rate

How it works

At a 12% conversion rate you expect an order roughly every 8.3 clicks, so about 17 clicks is two orders’ worth of opportunity. Below that, zero orders is noise, not evidence. Once there is enough data, the term’s ACoS is compared with your target to decide whether to scale it, keep it, trim the bid or cut it.

clicks needed = (100 ÷ conversion rate) × 2, rounded up ACoS = spend ÷ sales new bid = current CPC × (target ACoS ÷ ACoS)

Questions

Amazon PPC formulas, explained.

What is the breakeven ACoS formula?

Breakeven ACoS = profit per sale before advertising ÷ selling price. Profit before advertising is the price minus cost of goods, the Amazon referral fee and the FBA fee. If your ACoS is above this number, each ad sale loses money.

How do I calculate my maximum CPC on Amazon?

Max CPC = breakeven ACoS × selling price × conversion rate. The conversion rate matters because you pay for every click but only some clicks become orders. For a margin-safe bid, use your target ACoS instead of breakeven.

How many clicks before I pause a keyword with no sales?

A good rule is two expected orders’ worth of clicks: (100 ÷ your conversion rate) × 2. At a 10% conversion rate that is 20 clicks. Pausing earlier often kills terms that simply have not had enough traffic yet.
