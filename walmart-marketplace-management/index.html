Why sell beyond Amazon

Relying on one marketplace is a risk. A suppressed listing, a policy change or rising ad costs can cut your sales overnight. Selling on several channels spreads that risk and reaches buyers who never shop on Amazon. Walmart Marketplace has a large US audience and generally less advertising competition than Amazon, and TikTok Shop turns short videos directly into sales.

How we manage multichannel selling

Start from what already works. Your best Amazon products, keywords and images are the fastest way to launch on a new channel. We adapt them to each platform's rules and shoppers instead of starting from zero.

One strategy, many channels. Pricing, promotions and stock are planned together, so channels support each other rather than compete.

Content for each platform. Walmart shoppers search like Amazon shoppers. TikTok shoppers discover products through video. We prepare listings for search and short videos for social commerce, with help from our AI video team.

Profit on every channel. Each platform has different fees. We track margins per channel so you know where growth is actually profitable.

A dedicated specialist. A dedicated marketplace specialist is allocated to your account and reports to you every week.
