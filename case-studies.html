Case study · Amazon UK

UK brand: +64% year-on-year growth

The brief: the founder wanted steady, profitable growth and less time spent on day-to-day advertising decisions.

What we did:

  • Launched Sponsored Products campaigns in February, ahead of the spring season
  • Built a new Sponsored Products plan every quarter to keep sales growing
  • Took over every PPC decision: bids, bleeding keywords and placement adjustments
  • Ran Sponsored Brands Video and Sponsored Brands Headline campaigns
  • Weekly search-term harvesting reports to move winners into exact match
  • Set up and ran Best Deals for peak periods
  • Tracked true profit and margins in Sellerboard

How we worked: every new campaign was approved by the founder first, and a detailed review was prepared whenever requested.

Result: +64% year-on-year growth, while the founder handed the advertising workload over to us.
