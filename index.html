Questions

Amazon PPC questions sellers ask first.

How do I lower my ACoS on Amazon?

Start with the search term report. Add negative keywords for terms that spend without selling, move proven search terms into exact-match campaigns, set bids by product margin, and fix listings that get clicks but no orders. Our free audit shows exactly where your ACoS is leaking.

What is a good ACoS for Amazon sellers?

It depends on your margin. Your break-even ACoS equals your profit margin before ad spend, so a product with a 35% margin breaks even at 35% ACoS. Launch campaigns can run higher; mature products should sit well below break-even. We set targets per product, not one number for the whole account.

How much does Amazon PPC management cost?

It depends on your ad spend, number of products and marketplaces. After the free audit you get a fixed monthly quote before any work starts, with no long contract.

Do you work with small Amazon sellers and new product launches?

Yes. Many of our clients are small and mid-size brands. For launches we build the keyword plan, listing and launch campaigns together, so early ad spend builds rank instead of being wasted.

How long does Amazon PPC optimization take to show results?

Wasted spend usually drops within the first two to four weeks once negatives and bids are fixed. A full campaign restructure and listing improvements typically show their full effect over 60 to 90 days.

How should I prepare my Amazon ads for Black Friday?

Check stock early, refresh listings and images, set up deals and coupons, and raise budgets on proven keywords before CPCs climb in late November. During peak week, monitor bids and budgets daily. Our Q4 playbook above covers each step.

Am I hiring one person or an agency?

You talk to Haris, founder and MS in Project Management, on every call and every report. Specialists in PPC, design, development and video do the production under his review.

What does the free audit include?

A review of campaigns, listings and account health, with a ranked list of fixes and what each is worth. No obligation to continue.

Do you need full access to my Seller Central?

No. We start with read-only reports. Write access is added only after you approve the plan.

Which markets do you work in?

US, UK, EU and Australia on Amazon, plus Walmart, eBay, TikTok Shop and Shopify.

Can you generate B2B leads for my business?

Can you build a custom tool for my workflow?

Yes. On request, our development team builds custom tools such as data extraction apps, scrapers and CRM automations, scoped with a fixed price before work starts.

Can I hire you just for a website or a video?

Yes. Design, development and video are scoped and priced as separate projects.
