Good design is invisible

When an app or dashboard is well designed, users do not notice the design. They simply get things done. When it is not, they hesitate, make mistakes and leave. UI/UX design is about removing that friction: clear navigation, readable screens and flows that match how people think.

Our design process

1. Discover. We start with your goals, your users and your competitors. What should a user achieve, and what stops them today?

2. Structure. We map the user flows and create wireframes for every key screen. This is the cheapest stage to change your mind, so we get it right here.

3. Design. We turn wireframes into polished screens with a consistent design system: colours, typography, buttons, forms and states such as loading and errors.

4. Prototype and test. A clickable prototype lets you and your users try the product before a single line of code is written.

5. Hand off. Developers receive organised files with every measurement and component they need. We can also build the product ourselves through our web development service.
