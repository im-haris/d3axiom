Why most Amazon ad accounts lose money quietly

Amazon advertising rarely fails all at once. It leaks. A few search terms spend every day without selling. Bids stay high on keywords that stopped converting months ago. Top of Search gets a large adjustment even though the product converts better on product pages. Each problem looks small, but together they are often the difference between advertising that builds profit and advertising that eats it.

Good PPC management is less about clever tricks and more about consistent, data-led decisions every week. That is the work we take off your plate.

How we manage your Amazon PPC

We start with your numbers, not ours. Before changing a single bid, we calculate your break-even ACoS for each main product: price minus cost of goods, Amazon fees and fulfilment. That tells us the most you can afford to pay for a sale, and every bid follows from it. You can check the maths yourself with our free breakeven ACoS and max CPC calculator.

We give keywords a fair chance. A term with five clicks and no sales is not a failure yet. We use clear data thresholds before pausing or cutting anything, so we do not kill keywords that were about to work. When a term has had enough clicks and still does not sell, we act quickly.

We plan ahead of the season. Ad costs rise around Black Friday, Christmas and other peaks. We build budgets, deals and campaigns in advance, so your best keywords are not capped at the moment shoppers are buying.

You stay in control. New campaigns and bigger budget changes are approved by you first. If you want to review any decision in detail, we prepare it for you.
