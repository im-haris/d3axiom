When a custom tool makes sense

Off-the-shelf software covers common needs. But most businesses also have a few tasks that are specific to them: a weekly report built by hand, data copied from one system to another, or the same customer questions answered again and again. These tasks cost hours every week and invite mistakes. A small, well-built custom tool often pays for itself within months.

How we build

1. Map the work. We start by watching how the task is done today: the inputs, the steps, the decisions and the output. This shows exactly what to automate and what should stay with a person.

2. Scope and price. You receive a written scope with a fixed price and delivery date before any build starts.

3. Build in small steps. We deliver a working first version quickly, then improve it with your feedback in weekly demos.

4. Keep people in control. AI is used where it saves time, such as reading documents, sorting requests or drafting answers, with clear checks and an approval step where decisions matter.

5. Launch and support. We deploy the tool, train your team and stay available for updates.

Responsible data and AI

  • We respect each data source's terms of use and privacy laws
  • Your data stays yours, with access limited to the people who need it
  • AI outputs are checked and logged, so every action can be reviewed
