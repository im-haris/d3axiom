Why product video matters

Video shows what photos cannot: how big the product really is, how it works and how it feels to use. On Amazon, a good listing video helps shoppers decide faster. In Sponsored Brands Video ads, motion catches attention at the top of search results. On social media, short vertical videos are how most people discover new products.

How AI makes production faster

AI-assisted tools speed up editing, resizing, captioning and creating variations. That means you can test several versions of an ad instead of betting everything on one. Our team still controls the story, the brand look and the final quality, so videos feel professional rather than generic.

Our process

1. Brief. We agree the goal, the platform and the key message for each video.

2. Script and storyboard. A short plan for every scene, approved by you before production.

3. Production. We create the video from your footage, product images or a new shoot, using AI-assisted editing.

4. Variations. We resize and adapt the video for each platform, with captions and alternative openings for testing.

Responsible use of AI

  • We never show features a product does not have
  • We use licensed music, voices and assets
  • We follow platform rules for labelling realistic AI-generated content
